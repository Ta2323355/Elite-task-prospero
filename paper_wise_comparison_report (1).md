**Prepared:** 7 November 2026

The broad AI-LUS topic is already systematically reviewed. Pneumonia, pneumothorax and pleural effusion also have condition-specific reviews, and pleural effusion already has a published bivariate DTA meta-analysis. Therefore, the proposed project must not claim that no prior review exists.

The defensible potential contribution is a common DTA framework across four target conditions, plus prespecified care-setting, age, income-setting and external-validation comparisons. This is conditional on enough independent primary studies reporting compatible patient-level 2×2 data.

**Critical distinction:** Frontiers' 24 studies include segmentation, localization, object detection and feature-classification papers. Metrics such as DSC, IoU, mAP and frame-level sensitivity are not interchangeable with patient-level diagnostic sensitivity/specificity. These papers must not all be pooled as clinical DTA studies.

**Limitations:** The Lee six-study list is mapped from the six matching AI-LUS primary research papers cited in the article and needs a final check against the full-text inclusion table. Some exact DOIs and table-level metrics in Kamal/Marchi are flagged for verification. Scopus was not completed. PROSPERO per-record status was not retained in the supplied TXT. IEEE 135 records are preliminary title/abstract screening only.
