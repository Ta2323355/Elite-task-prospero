# Elite Task – PROSPERO Search and Research Novelty Assessment

## Project Overview

This repository documents the preliminary literature search, PROSPERO registration search, database search strategies, and research novelty assessment for the J84 Meta-Radiology research project.

The primary objective is to identify existing systematic reviews and meta-analyses related to Artificial Intelligence (AI) applications in Lung Ultrasound (LUS), assess potential overlap with the proposed review, and establish its research novelty.

## Research Topic

**Artificial Intelligence for Lung Ultrasound Interpretation: Diagnostic Accuracy and Clinical Applications**

## Objectives

- Identify existing systematic reviews and meta-analyses on AI-assisted lung ultrasound interpretation.
- Search PROSPERO for ongoing, completed, and discontinued registrations.
- Identify potentially overlapping reviews and assess their relevance.
- Search major academic databases for related publications.
- Document search strategies, search dates, and record counts.
- Support the assessment of research gaps and novelty.

## Search and Review Workflow

1. Develop and refine database-specific search strategies.
2. Run searches in the selected databases.
3. Record the exact search date and number of retrieved records.
4. Export search results wherever access permits.
5. Identify relevant systematic reviews and meta-analyses.
6. Examine PROSPERO registrations for potential overlap.
7. Compare existing reviews with the proposed research.
8. Document findings and update the research novelty assessment.

## Current Progress

**Status: In Progress**

Preliminary searches have been explored in PROSPERO, Scopus, IEEE Xplore, and Google Scholar.

Further direct database searches, verification of registration records, and documentation of final search counts are required before the search process can be considered complete.

No unverified record counts are treated as final results.

## Reproducibility

For transparency and reproducibility, each completed database search should include:

- Database name and platform
- Exact search string
- Search date
- Applied filters and restrictions
- Total records retrieved
- Exported result files, where available

## Important Note

This repository is maintained for research documentation and systematic review preparation. Search results and novelty conclusions must be verified against the original database records before final reporting.
